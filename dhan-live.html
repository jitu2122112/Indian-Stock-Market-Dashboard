⚠️ Risk & safety notes. This dashboard is for educational purposes and is not investment advice. Live trading of NSE equities and derivatives involves substantial risk of loss — you can lose your entire capital. Your Dhan Client ID and Access Token are stored only in this browser's localStorage and are sent only to api.dhan.co — or, if you fill in the optional API proxy field, to the proxy URL you entered, which forwards them to Dhan. Never enter a proxy you do not control yourself. Some browsers block direct calls to the Dhan API (CORS); in that case this page keeps working in paper mode. Deploy the Worker in proxy/ (see proxy/README.md) and paste its URL into the API proxy field to trade live. Public price feeds (Stooq / Yahoo) are delayed and unofficial. Always verify prices on your broker's terminal before trading.
