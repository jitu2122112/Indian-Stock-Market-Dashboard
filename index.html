📖 Trading Glossary

New to trading? Here are essential terms you should know:

Bull Market
A market that is rising and where the economy is sound.
Bear Market
A market in which prices are falling, encouraging selling.
Liquidity
How quickly you can buy or sell a stock without affecting its price.
Volatility
The rate at which the price of a stock increases or decreases.
Stop Loss
An order to sell a stock when it reaches a certain price to limit losses.
Demat Account
An account that holds your shares in electronic format.
NSE
National Stock Exchange - India's largest stock exchange.
BSE
Bombay Stock Exchange - Asia's oldest stock exchange.
Intraday Trading
Buying and selling stocks on the same day. All positions must be squared off before market closes.
Candlestick
A chart that displays the high, low, open, and close prices for a stock.
RSI
Relative Strength Index - A momentum indicator that measures the speed and change of price movements.
MACD
Moving Average Convergence Divergence - A trend-following momentum indicator.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much money do I need to start trading? +

You can start with as little as ₹1,000-₹5,000. However, we recommend starting with at least ₹50,000 to properly diversify and manage risk effectively. With smaller amounts, transaction costs can eat into your profits.

What is intraday trading? +

Intraday trading means buying and selling stocks on the same day. You must square off all positions before the market closes (3:15 PM IST). No delivery of shares happens in intraday trading, and you can use margin (leverage) to trade with more money than you have.

Can I trade on weekends? +

No, the Indian stock market (NSE and BSE) is closed on weekends (Saturday and Sunday) and public holidays. Trading hours are Monday to Friday, 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST. However, you can place AMO (After Market Orders) after 3:30 PM for the next trading day.

What is a stop loss and why is it important? +

A stop loss is an order to automatically sell a stock when it reaches a certain price. It's crucial for limiting your losses and protecting your capital from significant downside. Without a stop loss, a single bad trade can wipe out your entire account. Always use stop loss!

How do I choose which stocks to buy? +

Use our stock screener to find stocks that match your criteria. Consider factors like: fundamentals (P/E, ROE), technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages), sector trends, volume, and news. As a beginner, start with large-cap, liquid stocks like RELIANCE, TCS, INFY, HDFCBANK.

What is the best time to trade intraday? +

The best time for intraday trading is during the first hour (9:15 AM - 10:15 AM) when most of the day's movement happens. The last hour (2:30 PM - 3:30 PM) is also good for squaring off positions. Avoid trading during lunch hours (12:30 PM - 1:30 PM) as volumes are low.

How many stocks should I trade at once? +

As a beginner, we recommend trading only 1-2 stocks at a time. This helps you focus and understand the stock's behavior better. As you gain experience, you can increase this to 3-5 stocks. Never trade more than 5-10 stocks at once, as it becomes difficult to monitor all of them effectively.

What is the 1% rule in trading? +

The 1% rule means you should never risk more than 1% of your trading capital on a single trade. For example, if you have ₹1,00,000, you should risk only ₹1,000 per trade. This ensures that even if you have 10 losing trades in a row, you won't lose more than 10% of your capital.
