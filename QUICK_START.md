### **3. How Much to Buy?**

**Position Sizing Calculator:**

```
Your Capital: ₹1,00,000
Risk per trade (1%): ₹1,000
Risk per share: ₹25 (₹2,525 - ₹2,500)

Number of shares = ₹1,000 / ₹25 = 40 shares

Investment: 40 × ₹2,525 = ₹1,01,000
Max Loss: ₹1,000 (1% of capital)
Potential Profit: 40 × ₹50 = ₹2,000
```

**💡 Rule: Never risk more than 1-2% of your capital on a single trade!**

## 📈 **Your First Trade (Step-by-Step)**

### **Before You Start:**
- [ ] Completed all tutorials
- [ ] Opened a Demat account
- [ ] Have ₹10,000-₹20,000 capital
- [ ] Understand risk management

### **Step 1: Find a Signal**
1. Go to **Intraday Signals**
2. Look for a **BUY signal** with:
   - High confidence (>70%)
   - Low or Medium risk
   - Good risk-reward ratio (1:2 or better)

### **Step 2: Analyze the Stock**
1. Click on the stock name
2. Go to **Technical Analysis**
3. Check:
   - Is RSI between 30-70? (Not overbought/oversold)
   - Is MACD positive? (Bullish)
   - Is price above 50 & 200 DMA? (Bullish trend)
   - Are there bullish candlestick patterns?

### **Step 3: Calculate Position Size**
Use the formula:
```
Number of shares = (1% of capital) / (Entry - Stop Loss)
```

**Example:**
- Capital: ₹50,000
- 1% Risk: ₹500
- Entry: ₹1,000
- Stop Loss: ₹980
- Risk per share: ₹20
- Number of shares: ₹500 / ₹20 = **25 shares**

### **Step 4: Place the Trade**
1. Open your **trading platform** (Zerodha, Upstox, etc.)
2. Search for the stock
3. **Buy 25 shares at ₹1,000** (or market price)
4. **Set Stop Loss at ₹980**
5. **Set Target at ₹1,040** (or as per signal)

### **Step 5: Monitor & Exit**
- If price hits **₹1,040**: ✅ Take profit (₹1,000 profit)
- If price hits **₹980**: ❌ Stop loss triggered (₹500 loss - 1% of capital)
- **Never** hold beyond 3:15 PM (market square-off time)

## ⚠️ **5 Rules You MUST Follow**

### **Rule 1: Always Use Stop Loss**
❌ Never trade without a stop loss
✅ Always set stop loss before entering a trade

### **Rule 2: Risk Only 1-2% Per Trade**
❌ Don't risk ₹10,000 on a single trade
✅ Risk only ₹500-₹1,000 on a ₹50,000 capital

### **Rule 3: Follow the Trend**
❌ Don't fight the trend (don't buy in downtrend)
✅ "The trend is your friend"

### **Rule 4: Take Profits**
❌ Don't be greedy (don't hold for more profits)
✅ Take profits at your target

### **Rule 5: Cut Losses Early**
❌ Don't hope for a reversal
✅ Exit quickly if trade goes against you

## 💡 **Pro Tips for Beginners**

### **1. Start Small**
- Begin with **₹10,000-₹20,000** capital
- Trade **1-2 stocks** at a time
- Take **small positions** (1-2% of capital per trade)

### **2. Paper Trade First**
- Practice with **virtual money** before using real money
- Track your trades in a **journal**
- Learn from your **mistakes**

### **3. Best Times to Trade**
- **9:15-10:15 AM**: First hour (most movement)
- **10:15-12:30 PM**: Mid-day (less volatility)
- **12:30-1:30 PM**: Lunch hour (avoid trading)
- **1:30-3:00 PM**: Last hour (good for squaring off)

### **4. Stocks for Beginners**
Start with **large-cap, liquid stocks**:
- RELIANCE (Reliance Industries)
- TCS (Tata Consultancy)
- INFY (Infosys)
- HDFCBANK (HDFC Bank)
- ICICIBANK (ICICI Bank)
- HINDUNILVR (Hindustan Unilever)
- ITC (ITC Limited)
- BHARTIARTL (Bharti Airtel)

### **5. Common Mistakes to Avoid**
❌ Trading without stop loss
❌ Overleveraging (using too much margin)
❌ Chasing tips without analysis
❌ Revenge trading (trying to recover losses)
❌ Ignoring news and events
❌ Trading against the trend
❌ Holding losing trades
❌ Not taking profits

## ❓ **FAQ - Quick Answers**

### **Q: How much money do I need to start?**
A: You can start with **₹1,000-₹5,000**, but we recommend **₹50,000+** for proper diversification.

### **Q: Can I trade on weekends?**
A: **No**. Indian market is closed on weekends. Trading hours: **9:15 AM - 3:30 PM IST** (Mon-Fri).

### **Q: What is a stop loss?**
A: An **automatic order** to sell a stock when it reaches a certain price to **limit your losses**.

### **Q: How many stocks should I trade at once?**
A: As a beginner, **1-2 stocks** at a time. Never more than **5-10 stocks**.

### **Q: What is the best timeframe for intraday trading?**
A: **15-minute or 1-hour** charts are best for beginners. Avoid 1-minute charts (too noisy).

### **Q: How do I know when to buy/sell?**
A: Use **Intraday Signals** for buy/sell recommendations with entry, target, and stop loss.

### **Q: Can I make money every day?**
A: **No**. Trading has good and bad days. Focus on **consistent profits over time**, not daily wins.

### **Q: What is the 1% rule?**
A: **Never risk more than 1% of your capital on a single trade**. Example: ₹1,00,000 capital → Max risk per trade = ₹1,000.
