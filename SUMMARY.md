## 📊 **Example: Your First Trade**

### **Step 1: Find a Signal**
```
🎯 Intraday Signals → Active Signals

Stock: RELIANCE
Signal: BUY
Entry: ₹2,525
Target: ₹2,575
Stop Loss: ₹2,500
Risk: Medium
Confidence: 78%
```

### **Step 2: Calculate Position Size**
```
Your Capital: ₹1,00,000
Risk per trade (1%): ₹1,000
Risk per share: ₹25 (₹2,525 - ₹2,500)

Number of shares = ₹1,000 / ₹25 = 40 shares
```

### **Step 3: Place the Trade**
1. Open your broker's app (Zerodha, Upstox, etc.)
2. Search for **RELIANCE**
3. **Buy 40 shares at ₹2,525**
4. **Set Stop Loss at ₹2,500**
5. **Set Target at ₹2,575**

### **Step 4: Possible Outcomes**
- ✅ **Price hits ₹2,575**: Profit = 40 × ₹50 = **₹2,000**
- ❌ **Price hits ₹2,500**: Loss = 40 × ₹25 = **₹1,000** (1% of capital)

**Risk-Reward: 1:2 (Risk ₹1,000 to make ₹2,000)**

## ⚠️ **Important Rules to Follow**

### **The 5 Golden Rules**

1. **🛡️ Always Use Stop Loss**
   - Never trade without a stop loss
   - Protects your capital from big losses

2. **⚖️ Risk Only 1-2% Per Trade**
   - Example: ₹1,00,000 capital → Max risk = ₹1,000-₹2,000 per trade
   - Ensures you can survive losing streaks

3. **📈 Follow the Trend**
   - "The trend is your friend"
   - Don't fight the market

4. **💰 Take Profits**
   - Don't be greedy
   - Take profits at your target

5. **✂️ Cut Losses Early**
   - Don't hope for a reversal
   - Exit quickly if trade goes against you

### **Additional Rules**
- **Diversify**: Don't put all money in one stock
- **Don't Overtrade**: 2-3 trades per day max (as beginner)
- **Avoid Tips**: Don't trade based on tips without analysis
- **No Revenge Trading**: Don't try to recover losses quickly
- **Stay Disciplined**: Stick to your plan
